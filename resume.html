How I work

  • Define the bar before you build. A written threshold decides whether the work ships — the result, not the opinion, makes the call.
  • Check the licence before you build the baseline. On Aso Oke, half the architecture decisions came from licence checks; one early check avoided a launch blocker that would have permanently prevented charging for the product.
  • Say what doesn't work. Model cards and field reports are as explicit about the gaps as about the wins.
  • Ship things that work without a connection. Offline-first is a design decision, not an afterthought — it matters for cost, privacy and reach.
