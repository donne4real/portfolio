        How I build

        01 / MEASURE FIRST

        Pre-registered pass gates

        Write the quantitative ship threshold before training or coding. On Aso Oke, my own 8-epoch Whisper LoRA missed the gate at 61.4% WER — so zero-shot omniASR (32.0%) shipped instead.

        02 / LICENCE FIRST

        Audit rights in Week 1

        Verify commercial licences on models and datasets before building baselines. One Week-1 swap prevented a permanent commercial launch blocker.

        03 / REAL CONSTRAINTS

        Offline-first & radical honesty

        Ship for low-bandwidth devices, on-device privacy and zero cloud bloat — and publish known failure modes visibly in model cards.
